# Our claim checklist

Example for a made-up company, ExampleCo. Updated after the review of the checks up to November 10, 2026.

Last review: 2026-11-12

## Core claim
> We're the only expense tool that stops an out-of-policy purchase before it happens, not after.

Plain version for a non-technical reader: we block the bad purchase, others report it later.

## What would change our story
A competitor blocking out-of-policy payments at checkout, on any company card, generally available to all customers.

## Claim status
| Claim | Status | Since | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stops an out-of-policy purchase before it happens | Ours | 2026-10-06 | No competitor blocks payments at checkout on cards from other banks |
| Works on any company card, from any bank | Ours | 2026-10-06 | Competitor B's controls work on its own cards only |
| Policy templates ready to use | Shared with competitors | 2026-11-03 | Competitor B added free policy templates to its free plan, competitor-b.example/blog/templates |
| Set up in a day | Shared with competitors | 2026-10-30 | Competitor D: "live in under a day", competitor-d.example/pricing |

**Ours:** a reason to pick us. **Shared with competitors:** still true, still worth saying, but not a reason to pick us. **Retired:** don't use it.

## Expired claims: never use
- "The only expense tool with policy templates": retired 2026-11-03, Competitor B offers them free.

## Known gaps
1. Customer proof in retail: Competitor F named two retail customers we also pitch (2026-11-10). We have no public retail case study.

## Open questions
