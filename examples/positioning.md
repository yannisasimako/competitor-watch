# Positioning

Example for a made-up company, ExampleCo. Same company as the screenshots in the README.

## What we are, and who it's for
- **Company:** ExampleCo, expense management software.
- **Category:** spend control.
- **Target segment:** finance teams at US companies with 200 to 2,000 employees, where people buy on company cards every day.

## The problem
Finance finds out about an out-of-policy purchase weeks later, in an expense report. By then the money is spent, and the conversation with the employee is awkward for everyone.

## Alternatives: what buyers use instead of us
| Alternative | What it is | Why buyers pick it | Where it falls short for our segment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Competitor A | All-in-one finance suite | Already bought for accounting | Flags policy breaks after the purchase |
| Competitor B | Corporate card with built-in controls | Free cards, rewards | Controls only work on its own cards |
| Competitor D | Lightweight expense app | Cheap, set up in a day | No policy controls beyond receipts |
| Spreadsheets and email | Manual approvals | No new tool to buy | Slow, nothing stops a purchase |

## Differentiation
- **Core claim:** "We're the only expense tool that stops an out-of-policy purchase before it happens, not after."
- **Supporting claims:** works on any company card, from any bank · policy templates ready to use · set up in a day.
- **Must-haves:** receipt capture, accounting sync, mobile app.
- **What we are not:** a corporate card, an accounting system.
